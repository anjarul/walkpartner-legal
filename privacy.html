WalkPartner Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WalkPartner ("WalkPartner," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

Public URL: https://anjarul.github.io/walkpartner-legal/privacy.html

1. Who we are

WalkPartner is a walking-companion app that helps people find company for walks, coordinate meetups, message accepted partners or friends, and optionally track daily steps. The Service is designed for real-world walking meetups and community safety—not dating.

Privacy contact: anjarul.dev@gmail.com

If you operate WalkPartner through a registered company, include:

  • Legal entity name
  • Registered / mailing address
  • Country of establishment

2. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed through:

  • The WalkPartner mobile app
  • Backend services that power accounts, profiles, walks, messages, and safety tools
  • Related support communications

It does not cover third-party websites, maps providers, or Google/Apple account settings outside our control.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account and identity

When you sign in with Google, we receive (via Google Sign-In / OAuth):

  • Google account email address
  • Display name (if provided by Google)
  • Profile photo URL (if provided by Google)
  • Authentication tokens needed to create and maintain your session

We store a WalkPartner profile linked to your account, which may include:

  • Display name
  • Username (typically your email)
  • Email address
  • Optional phone number you choose to add (used for friend search; may be unverified)
  • Profile photo you upload or import
  • Gender (if you set it)
  • Bio (if you set it)
  • Trusted contact name and phone (if you set them for safety)
  • Account flags such as verification or suspension status
  • Walk streak and related activity counters derived from your use of the Service

3.2 Walk posts and meetup details

When you create or join a walk, we collect:

  • From / to place text (and optional linked area records)
  • Start and end times
  • Visibility (everyone or friends only)
  • Gender preference for partners
  • Max partners
  • Meeting point and notes
  • Join requests, accept/reject status, and membership roles
  • Ratings / reviews you submit about hosts or partners after walks
  • Trip comments (where available)

3.3 Location information

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Place labels you type or select for walk routes and meeting points
  • Map coordinates when you pick a place on the map
  • Approximate device location when you use "current location" / map features (with your permission)

We ask you not to post exact home addresses. Prefer public meeting points.

Location access on the device is used to help you pick places. Continuous background tracking for advertising is not part of WalkPartner's design.

3.4 Messages

We store:

  • Trip chat messages between walk participants after a join is accepted
  • Direct messages between users who are friends

Messages are content you create and are processed to deliver the chat features and for safety/abuse review when needed.

3.5 Social graph and safety signals

We process:

  • Friend requests and friendships
  • Blocks
  • Safety reports (reason, details, seriousness, related walk if provided)
  • Moderation outcomes (for example temporary suspension after serious reports)

3.6 Steps and health-related activity data

If you enable auto steps / pedometer features, we may process:

  • Daily step counts
  • Derived distance and calorie estimates shown in the app
  • Related local sensor / permission status on your device

This information is used to show your walking records and challenges—not to provide medical advice.

3.7 Device and notifications

If push notifications are enabled for your build/environment, we may collect:

  • Device push token
  • Platform type (for example Android)

We also store limited on-device preferences (for example language UI choice, whether you dismissed the optional phone prompt) using local app storage.

3.8 Technical and diagnostics data

Our infrastructure and SDKs may process standard technical data such as:

  • App version / request metadata needed to operate APIs
  • Authentication session data
  • Basic logs for security, debugging, and reliability

We do not sell this data.

3.9 Information from third parties

  • Google: identity/auth data for Sign-In
  • Map / geocoding providers (for example OpenStreetMap Nominatim): place search and reverse-geocoding responses when you use map/place features
  • Hosting / backend providers that store and process Service data on our behalf (see Section 5)

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  1. Create and secure your account
  2. Show profiles, walks, friends, requests, and ratings
  3. Match and coordinate walking partners
  4. Deliver trip chat and friend messaging
  5. Provide optional step history and challenges
  6. Enforce safety rules (blocks, reports, suspensions)
  7. Prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
  8. Improve reliability, performance, and product quality
  9. Communicate service-related notices (for example security or policy updates)
  10. Comply with law and respond to lawful requests

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your walk, message, or steps data to train third-party advertising profiles.

5. How we share information

5.1 With other users (by design of the Service)

Depending on your settings and actions, other users may see:

  • Your display name, photo, and profile details you choose to share
  • Walks you post as "Everyone," or "Friends only" (visible to accepted friends)
  • Join requests you send, and membership on walks you join
  • Messages you send in trip chat or friend chat
  • Ratings/reviews you submit

5.2 Service providers (processors)

We use trusted providers to operate WalkPartner, which may include:

Provider / category Purpose
Supabase (database, auth, file storage) Account, profile, walks, messages, media storage
Google Sign-In / Google Cloud OAuth Authentication
Firebase Cloud Messaging (if enabled) Push notifications
OpenStreetMap Nominatim (or similar) Place search / geocoding
Device OS / app stores Distribution, crash/diagnostics if you opt in at OS level

These providers process data under their terms and only as needed to provide their services to us.

5.3 Safety, legal, and business transfers

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it is necessary to:

  • Protect users from harm
  • Investigate abuse or fraud
  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • Protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property

If WalkPartner is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to ongoing confidentiality commitments.

5.4 What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not share personal data with third parties for their independent cross-app advertising
  • We do not make your exact live GPS track public as a social feed feature

6. Legal bases (where applicable)

If a privacy law requires a "legal basis," we typically rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you request (account, walks, messaging)
  • Legitimate interests: safety, security, abuse prevention, product improvement (balanced against your rights)
  • Consent: device permissions (location, activity recognition / sensors, notifications) and optional fields you choose to provide
  • Legal obligation: when we must retain or disclose data under law

7. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including:

  • Account & profile data: while your account remains active
  • Walks, memberships, ratings, reports: while needed for the Service and safety history
  • Messages: while needed to provide chat and for abuse investigation
  • Steps history: while your account remains active (or until you delete related data / account)
  • Backups / logs: for a limited period for security and disaster recovery

When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account within a reasonable period, except where we must retain information for safety, dispute resolution, or legal compliance (for example unresolved serious reports).

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data, including:

  • Authenticated access to APIs
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access controls and row-level security on backend data where configured
  • Limited employee/operator access on a need-to-know basis

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a secure device and protect access to your Google account.

9. Children's privacy

WalkPartner is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital-consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

In regions where a higher age is required for social meetup apps, you must meet that age to use WalkPartner.

10. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries where our servers or providers operate (for example the region selected for our Supabase project and the regions used by Google/Firebase). Those countries may have different data-protection laws than your home country. We take steps designed to protect your information consistent with this Policy.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account / personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (location, sensors, notifications) in your device settings
  • Export a copy of certain data

In-app controls

  • Edit profile information in Account / Edit profile
  • Skip or later add optional phone number
  • Choose walk visibility (Everyone / Friends only)
  • Block users
  • Disable location / activity permissions in Android settings
  • Sign out

Account deletion

Email anjarul.dev@gmail.com from your account email with the subject line Account deletion request. We will verify the request and delete or anonymize eligible data. You may also request deletion through any in-app deletion flow we provide.

Google account

You can revoke WalkPartner's Google Sign-In access in your Google Account permissions. That does not automatically delete WalkPartner server-side data—submit an account deletion request as above.
